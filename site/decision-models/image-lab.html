Visual Decisions.
Pixels to Probabilities.

Test multimodal System One models on images. Upload screenshots, receipts, or robotics feeds, edit typed question rubrics, and inspect decision distributions.

Multimodal System One: Traditional vision-language models decode multi-token descriptive prose character-by-character (taking 1.8–4.8 s and costing $0.35–$2.06 per 1k). Visual decision models project image tokens directly into bounded option logits in a single forward pass—achieving 0.26–0.71 s latency and $0.0074–$0.063 per 1,000 decisions on specialized Jev-class vision deciders.
